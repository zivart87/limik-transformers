    What each unit is built from

    Printed in the flyer Baseline 36,300 × 13,660 mm (119.1 × 44.8 ft). Zones 01 to 08. N+1 chiller-driven cooling on a closed loop. 1.77 MW IT envelope: 8 compute racks at 200 kW, 4 network racks. 2.2 MW cooling. 480 V service.

    Scaled from its diagram Zones 01 to 04 about 18.0 m long, five module widths across. Mechanical module about 3.1 m wide. Chillers about 3.1 × 10.6 m, 1.4 m apart. Headers on the zone 04 edge.

    Assumed Heights (deck 1.3 m, modules 3.6 m, chillers 2.8 m), door landing and stairs, the rack row, transformer and switch positions. Module length is open with Armada: the rendering would also suit 45 ft ISO modules inside the same footprint.
