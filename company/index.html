Our Story

Built on Engineering.
Driven by Precision.

Founded in 1994, LIMIK International is an American large power transformer manufacturer — purpose-built for utilities, independent power producers, EPC contractors, and defense infrastructure across the Americas.

With 25 years of engineering experience, we design and manufacture Power Transformers, Autotransformers, and Generator Step-Up units from 20 to 600 MVA, up to 525 kV. Every unit is engineered to specification — built to IEEE C57 family, ANSI, and NEMA standards, with full Buy American and BABA compliance on every order.

We build one product. We build it at the highest level. And we build it in the USA.

The U.S. imports 80% of its large power transformers.

Section 303 of the Defense Production Act authorizes the U.S. government to prioritize domestic production of materials critical to national security. LIMIK holds §303 eligibility — meaning DOD and DOE agencies can award transformer contracts directly to LIMIK, bypassing the standard multi-year competitive procurement cycle.
