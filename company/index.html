Our Story

Built on Engineering.
Driven by Precision

LIMIK builds transformers in the heavy utility class — up to 525 kV: Power Transformers, Autotransformers, and Generator Step-Up units. Equipment at this scale leaves no room for approximation. It demands deep engineering, serious production infrastructure, and absolute discipline in execution — and that is what stands behind every LIMIK unit.

There is no standard model here. Each unit begins with the customer's specification — power rating, voltage class, impedance, regulation — and is developed as its own project. IEEE C57, ANSI, and NEMA are not claims we advertise; they are the baseline we design from. Buy American and BABA compliance comes with every order.

We build for the grid. We build at the highest level. And we build in the USA.

How We Work

From First Drawing to Energization

A large power transformer is not a purchase — it is a multi-year relationship. That is why LIMIK customers work directly with the engineers responsible for their project: through design review, through factory acceptance testing, through delivery to site. No layers between you and the people building your unit. No distance between the question and the answer.

Our responsibility does not end at the loading dock. Commissioning support, complete documentation, and technical consultation follow every unit into the field — because equipment designed to run for decades deserves a manufacturer that stands behind it just as long.
