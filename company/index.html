Our Story

Built on Engineering.
Driven by Precision.

Founded in 1994, LIMIK International is an American large power transformer manufacturer — purpose-built for utilities, independent power producers, EPC contractors, and defense infrastructure across the Americas.

With 25 years of engineering experience, we design and manufacture Power Transformers, Autotransformers, and Generator Step-Up units from 20 to 600 MVA, up to 525 kV. Every unit is engineered to specification — built to IEEE C57, ANSI, and NEMA standards, with full Buy American and BABA compliance on every order.

We build one product. We build it at the highest level. And we build it in the USA.

Defense Production Act · Section 303

The One Designation
No Other New Entrant
Can Match

Section 303 of the Defense Production Act authorizes the U.S. government to prioritize domestic production of materials critical to national security. LIMIK holds §303 eligibility — meaning DOD and DOE agencies can award transformer contracts directly to LIMIK, bypassing the standard multi-year competitive procurement cycle.

DOD & DOE Direct Procurement

Defense and energy agencies can source LIMIK transformers under sole-source authority — bypassing the 2–4 year standard procurement cycle for qualifying critical infrastructure projects.

National Security Supply Chain

Military bases, DOE facilities, and critical grid interconnects qualify for §303 procurement. LIMIK is one of very few transformer suppliers eligible for this designation.

Buy American Compliance — By Default

Every LIMIK transformer is designed, engineered, and manufactured in the United States — satisfying Buy American Act and Berry Amendment requirements automatically on every order.

LIMIK's defense business is led by Ken Caward, with direct relationships across DOD procurement offices and U.S. Army Corps of Engineers infrastructure programs.
