## 8. Specifying a Transformer

**Section H2:** 
Specifying a Power Transformer for Defense Installations

**Intro Paragraph:** 
Replacing or expanding power capacity at a federal site requires balancing immediate load demands with long-term resilience and strict procurement compliance. Our engineering team reviews the following core criteria for military and government projects.

**Card 1:**
*H3:* MVA / kVA Rating & Headroom
*P:* Present base load, forecasted expansion of military facilities, and peak demand cycles for mission-critical operations. We size units to ensure sufficient thermal headroom for emergency microgrid operations without accelerating insulation degradation.

**Card 2:**
*H3:* Voltage Class & BIL
*P:* Primary and secondary voltages matched to secure transmission feeds and on-base distribution networks. Basic Impulse Level (BIL) ratings are specifically engineered for high survivability against lightning strikes and severe switching surges on the local grid.

**Card 3:**
*H3:* Cooling Classification & Signatures
*P:* ONAN, ONAF, or ODAF. We configure cooling systems to accommodate high ambient temperatures and harsh environments, while also factoring in strict noise ordinances or acoustic signature limitations required by specific installation guidelines.

**Card 4:**
*H3:* Physical Security & Hardening
*P:* We incorporate robust environmental hardening into the mechanical design. This includes heavy-duty tank construction, elevated seismic withstand bracing, tamper-resistant control cabinets, and specialized anti-corrosion coatings for coastal naval facilities.

**Card 5:**
*H3:* Retrofit & Footprint Matching
*P:* For legacy site upgrades, physical dimensions are non-negotiable. We design the footprint, phase spacing, and bushing layouts to drop seamlessly onto existing concrete pads, minimizing civil construction and the time civilian contractors spend inside the wire.
