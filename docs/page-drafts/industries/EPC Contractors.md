## 8. Specifying a Transformer

**Section H2:** 
Specifying Equipment for Turnkey Infrastructure

**Intro Paragraph:** 
Aligning transformer specifications with the broader site engineering plan is critical for fixed-price contracts. Our engineers review the following criteria to ensure seamless integration with your civil, electrical, and control system designs.

**Card 1:**
*H3:* Dimensional & Weight Constraints
*P:* We engineer the physical footprint, phase spacing, and overall weight to perfectly match your site's concrete foundation designs and the logistical limits of your heavy hauling and rigging contractors.

**Card 2:**
*H3:* Voltage & Impedance Targets
*P:* Primary and secondary voltages, along with strict impedance values, are custom-engineered to meet the specific grid interconnection agreements and short-circuit requirements defined in your electrical studies.

**Card 3:**
*H3:* Loss Evaluation Optimization
*P:* We balance upfront capital costs with long-term operational efficiency, optimizing core and copper designs to meet the specific No-Load and Load Loss evaluation formulas required by your end-client.

**Card 4:**
*H3:* Cooling & Acoustic Signatures
*P:* Radiator banks and cooling stages (ONAN/ONAF) are configured not only for thermal performance but to respect site boundary constraints and strict acoustic ordinances required by local permitting.

**Card 5:**
*H3:* Ancillary & SCADA Integration
*P:* Control cabinets and monitoring sensors are designed to interface seamlessly with your facility's SCADA or Building Management Systems (BMS), ensuring rapid and trouble-free commissioning on site.

## 11. FAQ

**Section H2:** 
EPC Procurement & Engineering FAQ

**Intro Paragraph:** 
Clear answers regarding lead times, logistics, and engineering processes for EPCs managing critical infrastructure projects.

**Q1:** What lead times can EPC contractors expect for large power transformers?
**A1:** While the global industry average currently ranges from 120 to 150 weeks, LIMIK maintains a target lead time of 48 to 72 weeks. We achieve this by strictly allocating our U.S. manufacturing capacity to committed North American projects.

**Q2:** How does LIMIK coordinate delivery with active construction sites?
**A2:** We understand that heavy lifts require perfect timing. Our logistics team works directly with your site superintendents and heavy hauling contractors to align the delivery of the transformer precisely with your foundation readiness and crane availability.

**Q3:** Do you allow our quality assurance teams and end-clients to attend FAT?
**A3:** Yes. We maintain an open-door policy at our U.S. facility. We encourage EPC contractors and their clients to attend Factory Acceptance Testing (FAT) in person to verify electrical and mechanical performance before the unit ships.

**Q4:** Can you design transformers to match existing footprints for upgrade projects?
**A4:** Yes. For brownfield sites and infrastructure upgrades, we custom-engineer the physical footprint, phase spacing, and bushing layouts to drop seamlessly onto existing concrete pads, minimizing the need for new civil construction.

**Q5:** Do your transformers meet Buy American Act (BAA) and IRA requirements?
**A5:** Yes. Every LIMIK transformer is 100% engineered and manufactured in the United States. This inherently satisfies BAA requirements and significantly helps renewable energy developers meet the thresholds for the IRA Domestic Content Bonus.

**Q6:** How does the engineering and procurement process start?
**A6:** The process begins with a direct engineering review of your technical specifications, single-line diagrams, and site constraints. We resolve design details directly with your team, followed by a Letter of Intent (LOI) to secure your production slot ahead of the formal purchase order.
