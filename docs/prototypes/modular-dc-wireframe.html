What is the lead time for LIMIK Core?

The module ships 12 weeks after design approval. The medium-voltage transformer is built on our own line in parallel, so both arrive on one coordinated schedule.

What site preparation is required?

A level concrete pad, a medium-voltage power source and network access. Modules arrive tested and connect through pre-engineered interconnects.

Can it run on behind-the-meter power?

Yes. LIMIK Core runs on grid power, on-site generation or both. We also build the step-up transformers that connect on-site generation.

Which GPUs are supported?

The platform is chip-agnostic: NVIDIA Blackwell and Vera Rubin, AMD Instinct, Google TPU and Cerebras. Rack density is engineered to your configuration.

Can the module be relocated later?

Yes. Enclosures use standard high-cube dimensions for U.S. rail and highway transport and can be redeployed.

Who manufactures the power transformer?

LIMIK does. We build medium- and high-voltage transformers up to 600 MVA and 525 kV, and LIMIK Core is engineered around them.
