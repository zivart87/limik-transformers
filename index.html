About Us

Engineering That Powers the Grid

Founded in 1994, LIMIK designs and manufactures large power transformers — Power Transformers, Autotransformers, and Generator Step-Up units — for utilities, independent power producers, and EPC contractors across the Americas. Every unit is custom-engineered from 20 to 600 MVA, up to 525 kV.
