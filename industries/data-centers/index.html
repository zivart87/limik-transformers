Compute Demand Is Rewriting the Power Curve

The rise of AI training clusters, high-density GPU workloads, and always-on cloud infrastructure has shifted data center power profiles from tens of megawatts to hundreds — and now to gigawatt-scale campuses. New facilities are being planned five to seven years out. Substation transformers, autotransformers, and generator step-up units sit at the top of every long-lead procurement list.

LIMIK is building American production capacity aligned with this timeline. For owners, developers, and EPC contractors planning their next campus or expansion, that means a manufacturer with slots to allocate — not a queue to join.
