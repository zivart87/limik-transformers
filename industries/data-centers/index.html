Context

Compute Demand Is Rewriting the Power Curve

The rise of AI training clusters, high-density GPU workloads, and always-on cloud infrastructure has shifted data center power profiles from tens of megawatts to hundreds — and now to gigawatt-scale campuses. New facilities are being planned five to seven years out. Substation transformers, autotransformers, and generator step-up units sit at the top of every long-lead procurement list.

LIMIK is building American production capacity aligned with this timeline. For owners, developers, and EPC contractors planning their next campus or expansion, that means a manufacturer with slots to allocate — not a queue to join.

Engineered for Mission-Critical Duty

Uptime-Focused Engineering

Every unit is designed for continuous, full-load operation with conservative thermal margins and rigorous quality control. Downtime is not acceptable in this segment — and it is not designed in. Design targets prioritize high mean time between failures (MTBF) over the unit's operating life.

Low-Loss Performance

Optimized core and winding designs to minimize no-load and load losses. Lower losses mean less heat, less cooling burden, and better facility-level energy metrics over decades of continuous operation.

Harmonic-Tolerant Design

Data center loads generate significant harmonic content through UPS systems, VFDs, and IT power supplies. Our units are engineered with appropriate impedance, insulation systems, and thermal derating to handle non-linear load profiles.

Scalable Capacity

Ratings up to 600 MVA and 525 kV support facility sizes from single-hall colocation up to gigawatt-scale AI campuses. Multi-phase deployments and campus master plans can be served from one manufacturer.

Standards-Based Compliance

Designed to IEEE, ANSI, NEMA, and IEC standards. Buy American compliant. Documentation packages prepared for the audit trails hyperscale procurement teams require.

Custom-Engineered per Site

No two data center sites are identical. Voltage classes, footprint constraints, connection geometries, cooling classifications, and impedance targets are engineered to your specification — not selected from a catalog.
