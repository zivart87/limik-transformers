Engineered for National Security

Islandable Microgrid Integration

Energy independence is critical for military readiness. As federal installations move away from reliance on vulnerable commercial grids, our generator step-up (GSU) and substation transformers are engineered specifically to anchor isolated microgrids. When the primary utility feed is compromised due to extreme weather, cyber events, or physical attacks, our units seamlessly handle the transition to on-base generation. They are designed to absorb sudden load steps and harmonic distortions associated with localized, independent power sources. This level of engineering ensures uninterrupted power for mission-critical command centers, radar installations, and strategic communications facilities, maintaining operational continuity under the most severe contingency scenarios.

What Matters

Specifying a Power Transformer for Defense Installations

Replacing or expanding power capacity at a federal site requires balancing immediate load demands with long-term resilience and strict procurement compliance. Our engineering team reviews the following core criteria for military and government projects.

MVA / kVA Rating & Headroom

Present base load, forecasted expansion of military facilities, and peak demand cycles for mission-critical operations. We size units to ensure sufficient thermal headroom for emergency microgrid operations without accelerating insulation degradation.

Voltage Class & BIL

Primary and secondary voltages matched to secure transmission feeds and on-base distribution networks. Basic Impulse Level (BIL) ratings are specifically engineered for high survivability against lightning strikes and severe switching surges on the local grid.

Cooling Classification & Signatures

ONAN, ONAF, or ODAF. We configure cooling systems to accommodate high ambient temperatures and harsh environments, while also factoring in strict noise ordinances or acoustic signature limitations required by specific installation guidelines.

Physical Security & Hardening

We incorporate robust environmental hardening into the mechanical design. This includes heavy-duty tank construction, elevated seismic withstand bracing, tamper-resistant control cabinets, and specialized anti-corrosion coatings for coastal naval facilities.

Retrofit & Footprint Matching

For legacy site upgrades, physical dimensions are non-negotiable. We design the footprint, phase spacing, and bushing layouts to drop seamlessly onto existing concrete pads, minimizing civil construction and the time civilian contractors spend inside the wire.
