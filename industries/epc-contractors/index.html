What Matters

Specifying Equipment for Turnkey Infrastructure

Aligning transformer specifications with the broader site engineering plan is critical for fixed-price contracts. Our engineers review the following criteria to ensure seamless integration with your civil, electrical, and control system designs.

Dimensional & Weight Constraints

We engineer the physical footprint, phase spacing, and overall weight to perfectly match your site's concrete foundation designs and the logistical limits of your heavy hauling and rigging contractors.

Voltage & Impedance Targets

Primary and secondary voltages, along with strict impedance values, are custom-engineered to meet the specific grid interconnection agreements and short-circuit requirements defined in your electrical studies.

Loss Evaluation Optimization

We balance upfront capital costs with long-term operational efficiency, optimizing core and copper designs to meet the specific No-Load and Load Loss evaluation formulas required by your end-client.

Cooling & Acoustic Signatures

Radiator banks and cooling stages (ONAN/ONAF) are configured not only for thermal performance but to respect site boundary constraints and strict acoustic ordinances required by local permitting.

Ancillary & SCADA Integration

Control cabinets and monitoring sensors are designed to interface seamlessly with your facility's SCADA or Building Management Systems (BMS), ensuring rapid and trouble-free commissioning on site.
